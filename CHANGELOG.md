# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-10-16)

The first release, for GNOME Shell 50.

- The Do Not Disturb switch at the bottom of the date menu, next to **Clear**.
- Do Not Disturb for a while: for 30 minutes, an hour, 8 hours, 2 days or
  until you turn it off. It turns itself off on time, even while the screen is
  locked.
- The Do Not Disturb icon next to the clock while it is on.
- No Do Not Disturb toggle and icon in the quick settings.
- In English and Russian.
